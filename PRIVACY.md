# Privacy Policy

**ProvisionQL** does not collect, store, or send any personal data to the
developer. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no user
accounts.

## Network connections

The app makes no network connections. Files you open are parsed entirely on
your Mac.

## Data stored on your Mac

Files you open, and everything read from them (bundle metadata, entitlements,
certificates, provisioning profiles), are processed locally and never leave
your device. Reading an app archive extracts parts of it into a temporary
folder on your Mac, which is deleted as soon as the file has been inspected.

Because nothing is sent to the developer, there is no personal data held on
any server, and no data deletion request is applicable.
